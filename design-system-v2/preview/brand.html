Brand Voice

DO
  • Professional & confident
  • Data-backed claims ("28% faster")
  • Action-first CTAs ("Generate JD")
  • "Powered by Claude AI"
  • "AI-powered" not "AI-driven"
  • Eyebrow → Heading → Body pattern
DON'T
  • Jargon-heavy or cold
  • "Revolutionary" / "Game-changing"
  • Emoji in product UI
  • Mix icon libraries
  • Colors outside the palette
  • Custom fonts — Inter only
PERSONALITY
  • Smart but not cold
  • Enterprise-grade but approachable
  • Modern and trustworthy
  • Data-informed, human-centred
  • Confident — not boastful
