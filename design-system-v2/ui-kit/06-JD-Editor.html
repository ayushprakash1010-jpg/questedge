Senior Product Manager — Platform
Bengaluru (Hybrid) Full-time 5–9 yrs experience ₹40–55 LPA

About the Role

We're hiring a Senior PM to own our core platform — the infrastructure layer powering 12M+ users across payments, lending, and insurance verticals. You'll partner with Engineering, Design, and Data to ship features that unlock growth for our business units.

What You'll Do

  • Define and execute the product strategy for platform capabilities including APIs, developer tooling, and internal services.
  • Translate ambiguous business problems into clear product requirements and acceptance criteria.
  • Partner with 4+ engineering pods to deliver high-quality, high-impact features on time.
  • Drive data-informed decisions using SQL, dashboards, and A/B test results — you're comfortable getting your hands dirty with data.
  • Own the platform roadmap, quarterly planning, and stakeholder communication from IC level to C-suite.
  • Mentor 1–2 APMs and establish best practices for product thinking across the org.

What We're Looking For

  • 5+ years of PM experience with at least 2 years on platform, API, or infrastructure products.
  • Strong quantitative skills — experience with metrics frameworks (HEART, AARRR) and statistical thinking.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication; ability to synthesise complex trade-offs for senior leadership.
  • Prior experience in fintech, payments, or lending is a strong plus.
  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a quantitative field; MBA from a Tier 1 school preferred.

Benefits

  • Competitive compensation: ₹40–55 LPA (base + equity)
  • ESOP with 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff
  • Comprehensive health insurance (self + family)
  • ₹1L annual learning & development budget
  • Hybrid work — 3 days in office (Bengaluru)
